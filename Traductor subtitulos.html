Translate your subtitles in three steps

  1. Get an API key. It works like a password for the AI and you pay per use: translating a 20-minute video usually costs a few cents. If you are not sure which one to pick, start with OpenRouter, which gives you many models with a single key.
  2. Drop your .srt or .vtt file. The app detects its language and suggests a target.
  3. Press “Translate”, review any lines you want and download the result.

Rather not pay anything? Run a model on your own computer with LM Studio. Your subtitles never leave your machine.

How it works

What it does

It sends your subtitles in batches to the AI you choose, together with the full transcript of the video as context, so the translation stays consistent from start to finish. Every subtitle keeps its timing and its line breaks.

What it costs

The app is free. You pay for the AI usage, directly to the provider, with your own key. You see an estimate before you start and the real cost while it translates. As a guide, a 20-minute video costs less than 1 cent with Claude Haiku or DeepSeek, about 5 cents with Gemini Flash and about 10–20 cents with Claude Sonnet. With LM Studio it is free.

Which model to choose

  • Quality and natural phrasing: Claude Sonnet or Gemini (high end).
  • Fast and cheap: Gemini Flash, Claude Haiku or DeepSeek.
  • Into Chinese: Qwen Max or Gemini.
  • Offline and free: LM Studio with Qwen or Gemma.

Privacy

This page has no server of its own, no sign-up and no analytics. Your keys are stored only in this browser. Subtitles go straight from your browser to the provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenRouter, OpenAI) or stay on your computer if you use LM Studio.

Tips

  • Describe the video in “Context and glossary”: who is speaking, what it is about and the tone.
  • Put terms and acronyms that must always be translated the same way in the glossary.
  • If you do not like the result, press “Clear translation” and try another model.
  • The ↻ button on each row translates just that line again.

API keys

You only need the one for the engine you are going to use. Keys are stored in this browser and never sent anywhere else.
